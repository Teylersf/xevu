# XEVU name screening

Screened 2026-10-29 with public web searches for exact names together with
software, security, encryption and operating-system terms. This is a working
product name, not a legal clearance or a claim of worldwide uniqueness.

Many short alternatives already have direct software identities. Examples found
include [Orva OS](https://www.orvaos.com/), [Oryvo](https://oryvo.ai/),
[Avyro](https://avyro.io/), [Kyvo](https://kyvo.website/) and
[Veylo](https://www.veylo.com/company). Those were not selected.

The searches did not surface an obvious same-name XEVU operating-system or
encrypted-file product. The term does appear elsewhere, including
[XEVU Limited in the UK company register](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/10028044)
and radio call signs. Those appearances mean exclusivity should not be assumed.
No trademark registry clearance, domain ownership or registration was performed.

Why the name fits the brief: four letters, two spoken syllables (zeh-voo), no
industry word or acronym, and an X-shaped identity suited to controlled movement
of files. It can cover creative, professional, academic and personal use without
promising a security capability that the preview does not have.
